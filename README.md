# reverse-recast-snowball
A Baby Step 6 mortgage payoff strategy using a sinking-fund floor, lump-sum principal payments, mortgage recasts, and redirected payment savings to accelerate becoming debt-free.

Part of why I’m sharing the Reverse Recast Snowball is to raise awareness about mortgage recasting. A recast isn’t right for everyone, but it’s a financial tool many homeowners don’t even know exists. After making a substantial principal payment, an eligible mortgage can be re-amortized over the remaining term, lowering the required monthly payment without refinancing. That lower required payment can provide flexibility, improve cash flow, or—as with this strategy—be redirected toward paying the mortgage off even faster.
